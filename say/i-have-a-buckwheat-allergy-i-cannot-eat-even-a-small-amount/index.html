How to say “I have a buckwheat (soba) allergy. I cannot eat even a small amount.” in Japanese

そばアレルギーです。
少しでも食べられません。
Soba arerugī desu. Sukoshi demo taberaremasen.
I have a buckwheat (soba) allergy. I cannot eat even a small amount.
